A private voice assistant for one person's calendar and email

the_swarm runs an AI assistant called Gary on its developer's own computer. Gary keeps the developer's calendar, email, notes and projects organised, and speaks with them by voice.

the_swarm is a personal, self-hosted project. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and the only Google accounts it connects to are the developer's own account and the assistant's own Gmail account.

What it does

  • Calendar. Lists, creates, moves and deletes events when asked, and schedules blocks of focused work around existing commitments.
  • Email. Reads and searches email when asked, announces new mail, and drafts replies and new emails. Nothing is sent until the developer hears it read back and confirms it, or approves it.
  • Planning. Plans the day three times a day from the developer's projects, tasks and free time, and keeps track of follow-ups and deadlines.
  • Notes. Writes notes and daily summaries to the developer's local Joplin notebook.

How it uses Google data

the_swarm uses Google Calendar and Gmail only to provide the features above to the developer. It keeps its data on the developer's own computer, does not sell or advertise with it, and does not use it to train AI models. The privacy policy lists exactly what is accessed, why, and who it is shared with.
