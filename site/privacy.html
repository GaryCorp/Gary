Privacy policy

Effective 21 September 2026

the_swarm is a personal, self-hosted voice assistant ("Gary") that its developer runs on their own computer. It connects only to the developer's own Google account and to the assistant's own Gmail account. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Google user data the_swarm accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how to remove it.

Google data the_swarm accesses

PermissionWhat it is used for
Basic profile and email address
openid, userinfo.email		 To identify which Google account signed in, so the developer's account and the assistant's mailbox are never confused.
Calendar events
calendar.events		 Developer's account only. To list events when asked, to create, move or delete events when asked, and to read busy times so work can be scheduled around existing commitments.
Read Gmail
gmail.readonly		 To list, search and read email when asked, to find a contact's address, and to announce new mail. the_swarm cannot change, label, archive or delete email.
Send Gmail
gmail.send		 To send replies and new emails, only after the developer has heard the message read back and confirmed it, or approved it.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to provide the assistant's features to the developer: answering questions about their calendar and email, drafting and sending email they confirm, scheduling their work, and planning their day. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles for anyone else.

  • When the developer asks about email or the calendar, the relevant details (for example sender, subject, a short preview, or the text of an email they ask to hear, or event titles and times) are sent to the AI model that runs the conversation.
  • The daily planning runs see the developer's busy times as start and end times only, with no event titles or attendees, and the sender, subject and a short preview of up to ten unread emails. Never full emails or email addresses.
  • The new-mail check reads only the sender and subject of new unread email. It runs entirely on the developer's computer and is not sent to any AI model.

Who the data is shared with

Google user data is shared only with OpenAI, through its API, as needed to run the conversation and planning features above. Requests are sent with storage turned off, and OpenAI's API terms state that API data is not used to train its models by default. Google user data is not shared with anyone else. Email content is removed from anything sent to other services the_swarm uses, such as GitHub.

Storage and security

  • the_swarm has no servers of its own. Everything runs on the developer's computer.
  • Google sign-in credentials are stored on that computer, encrypted, with the encryption key kept separately.
  • Email and calendar content is not copied into a database. The only email details kept are the sender names and subjects in the record of what the assistant has said out loud, stored locally.
  • The assistant's web pages are reachable only from the developer's own computer.

Limited Use

the_swarm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

  • Data from Google is kept only for as long as the assistant needs it to answer a request. Conversations are discarded when the assistant goes back to sleep.
  • To remove access at any time, open Google Account › Third-party connections and remove the_swarm. This stops all further access, and the stored sign-in on the developer's computer stops working.
  • To ask for any locally stored data to be deleted, email the contact address below.

Children

the_swarm is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
